## 📺 What It Is
IPTV Player for Windows is a free, smart IPTV player that turns your PC into a full streaming hub. Point it at an M3U playlist or your provider login, and it neatly sorts your live channels, movies, and series with an easy program guide. It is made for anyone who wants a clean, responsive player without a steep learning curve. Bring your own playlist and the app handles the rest, from channel lists to favorites and search.

## ⭐ Key Features
> A smart IPTV player that keeps live TV, VOD, and your guide in one tidy window.

- 📡 **M3U playlist loading** — Add channels from an M3U or M3U8 link or file in moments.
- 🎬 **Live TV, movies, and series** — Stream everything your subscription offers from one place.
- 🗓️ **Smart EPG guide** — Browse a program guide to see what is on now and coming up.
- ⭐ **Favorites and search** — Save go-to channels and find anything fast.
- 🔑 **Flexible logins** — Connect with a playlist URL or provider login details.
